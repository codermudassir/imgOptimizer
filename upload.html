Why local processing

The fastest server is the one that doesn't exist.

Instant by default

No queue, no upload progress bar. Your browser's own decoder runs the moment you drop a file.

Nothing to leak

Photos never reach a server, because there's no upload step in the first place — only your device ever sees the pixels.

Quality you control

Per-image sliders for quality and size, so the one product photo that matters can get a different setting than the rest.

Batch when you need it

Set a format once, apply it to every file, then zip the whole batch into a single download.
